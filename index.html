01 — The connection: The internet is a network of networks. Your phone sends a request through Wi-Fi or mobile data to your internet provider.

02 — The router: A router directs data toward its destination. It is like a digital traffic controller, choosing a path across connected networks.

03 — Packets: Information is split into small packets. Each packet carries addressing information so networks can move it independently.

04 — DNS: DNS translates a human-friendly domain name into an IP address, helping your device find the right server.

05 — The server: The server receives the request, finds the content and sends data back. Your browser assembles it into the page or video you see.

06 — Security: HTTPS encrypts data in transit. The network is powerful, but safe browsing, updates and strong passwords still matter.
